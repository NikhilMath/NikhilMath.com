Nikhil Math is an IT support manager, nonprofit board member and hobbyist game developer based in Utah. Nikhil runs IT at Visionary Homes, heads public relations on the board of Recovery Café Logan, and makes small browser games, including Rarest Catch and Keyboard Heist.

Career

Nikhil started out in web development. After finishing a full-stack web development certificate in Ruby on Rails and React in 2021, Nikhil joined Kimberbell in 2022 as a web developer and IT specialist, building accessible online stores. A promotion to IT manager followed in 2023, leading the company's web and IT team.

Since October 2023 Nikhil has been the IT Support Manager at Visionary Homes, looking after more than 100 employees on Mac, iPhone, iPad and Windows. The job includes running Jamf and Apple Business Manager, working with the company's outside IT provider, and building an onboarding system in Asana that covers more than 75 job titles, each with its own checklist.

Nikhil has also owned and managed a four-unit apartment building since 2016.

Community work

Nikhil sits on the board of directors of Recovery Café Logan, a nonprofit recovery community, as head of public relations, and served as board secretary from 2023 to 2024. Nikhil also chairs a local Nar-Anon support group.

While a student at Utah State University, Nikhil secured $240,000 in accessibility funding through independent advocacy and was elected to the student fee board, which represents nearly 30,000 students.

Games

Nikhil makes small games that run in the browser. Both are free to play, and both are on the video games page.

  • Keyboard Heist, a choose-your-own-adventure office comedy that opens with a printer ticket from the CEO. It has 42 scenes and four endings, only two of which are realistic.
  • Rarest Catch, a daily Pokémon game where every player gets the same five prompts, and a right answer scores more the fewer other players picked it.

Personal life

Off the clock, Nikhil rock climbs, plays Pokémon Go and 3D prints things that are actually useful. Nikhil is also a steady reader and logs every book on Goodreads, with tastes running from manga to personal finance.
